Cote de Pablo

Nov 12, 1979 Santiago, Chile ALMA Award Winner

The Early Journey

Born María José de Pablo Fernández, Cote moved from Chile to Miami at age 10. Her journey into the arts began at the New World School of the Arts, later leading her to Carnegie Mellon University. Before finding fame, she worked as a waitress in New York City while pursuing theater roles.

Career Breakthrough

In 2005, she landed the career-defining role of Ziva David on NCIS. Originally auditioning for a character with a different background, the producers were so impressed that they changed the character to an Israeli Mossad officer just 48 hours before filming. She became a global icon, performing many of her own stunts.

Professional Challenges

"I worked eight years crafting this character... when I felt she was not being treated with the respect she deserved, all the money in the world couldn't buy me."

In 2013, Cote made the difficult decision to leave NCIS at the height of its popularity. She cited creative concerns regarding the direction of her character's story, proving her commitment to artistic integrity over financial gain.

The Legacy

Beyond the screen, Cote is an accomplished singer and advocate. Her return to the NCIS universe in recent years was a historic television event, cementing her status as one of the most beloved actresses in procedural drama history.
