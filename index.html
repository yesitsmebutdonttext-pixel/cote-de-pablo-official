The Journey of an Icon

Maria José de Pablo Fernández, known professionally as Cote de Pablo, is a multi-talented Chilean-American actress and singer who defined a generation of television drama.

At age 10, Cote moved from Santiago to Miami, where she attended Arvida Middle School and later studied music theater at Carnegie Mellon University. Her early career saw her hosting the talk show Control alongside Carlos Ponce, but her global breakthrough came in 2005.

As Ziva David on the CBS series NCIS, she became an international household name, winning an ALMA Award for Favorite Television Actress. Beyond acting, Cote is a trained vocalist, performing a haunting rendition of "Temptation" for the NCIS soundtrack, showcasing her immense range.

Biography

Maria José de Pablo Fernández, known professionally as Cote de Pablo, is a Chilean-American actress who captivated global audiences.

Born on November 12, 1979, in Santiago, Chile, she moved to Miami at age 10. She attended Carnegie Mellon University to study music and theater, which paved the way for her incredible career. She is best known for her iconic role as Ziva David on NCIS, where she became a symbol of strength for fans worldwide.
