Cote de Pablo

Santiago, Chile November 12, 1979

Cote de Pablo is a Chilean-American actress and singer, best known for her iconic portrayal of Special Agent Ziva David on the hit CBS series NCIS. Her performance earned her an ALMA Award and solidified her place as a powerhouse in television drama.

Born in Santiago, Chile, she moved to the United States at age 10. She later attended Carnegie Mellon University, where she studied music and theater, appearing in several plays including The House of Bernarda Alba and Indiscretions.

Beyond her television success, de Pablo is a talented vocalist, notably performing a cover of Tom Waits' "Temptation" for an NCIS episode, showcasing her deep, soulful musical range.
