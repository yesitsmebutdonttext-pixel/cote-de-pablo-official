The Life Story

Maria José de Pablo Fernández, known professionally as Cote de Pablo, is a Chilean-American actress who captivated global audiences.

Born in 1979 in Santiago, Chile, she moved to Miami at a young age, later honing her craft at Carnegie Mellon University. Best known for her iconic portrayal of Ziva David on the CBS series NCIS, she has become a symbol of strength and talent in modern cinema.
