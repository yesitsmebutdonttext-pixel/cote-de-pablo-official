The Journey of an Icon

Maria José de Pablo Fernández, professionally known as Cote de Pablo, is an award-winning Chilean-American actress and singer who has left an indelible mark on television history.

Born in Santiago, Chile, Cote moved to the United States at the age of 10. She pursued her passion for the arts at Carnegie Mellon University, where she studied Music and Theater. Her early career saw her hosting the Latin-American talk show Control alongside Carlos Ponce, before moving into dramatic acting.

In 2005, she achieved global stardom when she was cast as Ziva David in the CBS series NCIS. Her portrayal of the complex, fierce Mossad officer earned her a massive international following and the 2011 ALMA Award for Favorite Television Actress.

Beyond her acting prowess, Cote is a classically trained vocalist. She famously performed a haunting rendition of Tom Waits' "Temptation" for an NCIS episode, later released on the official soundtrack. Most recently, she has returned to executive produce and star in the highly anticipated spinoff NCIS: Tony & Ziva, continuing the legacy of her most iconic role.
