The Life Story

Maria José de Pablo Fernández, known professionally as Cote de Pablo, is a Chilean-American actress who captivated global audiences.

Best known for her iconic portrayal of Ziva David on the CBS series NCIS, she has become a symbol of strength and talent in modern cinema. Born in Santiago, Chile, her journey from Carnegie Mellon University to international stardom is a testament to her versatile craft.
