Biography

María José de Pablo Fernández, known professionally as Cote de Pablo, is a renowned Chilean-American actress and singer. Born in Santiago, Chile, she moved to the United States at age 10, where she attended the New World School of the Arts to study musical theater.

After graduating from Carnegie Mellon University in 2000 with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Drama, she moved to New York City. Her early career featured roles in The $treet and The Jury, but her global breakthrough came in 2005 when she was cast as Ziva David in the police procedural drama NCIS. Her portrayal of the complex, fierce Mossad officer earned her an ALMA Award for Favorite Television Actress in 2011.

Beyond her acting, Cote is an accomplished vocalist. She performed a haunting rendition of Tom Waits' "Temptation" for NCIS and has been featured on various soundtracks, including the film The 33. In 2024, she returned to the screen alongside Michael Weatherly for the spinoff series NCIS: Tony & Ziva, where she also serves as an Executive Producer.
